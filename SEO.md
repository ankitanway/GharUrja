# SEO.md

## SEO Strategy for GharUrja

GharUrja should win through useful, high-intent, local solar content.

The goal is not to chase broad vanity keywords first.
The goal is to capture homeowners who are already close to a solar decision.

---

## Primary SEO Goals

1. Rank for local and state-specific solar queries.
2. Rank for solar calculator and subsidy questions.
3. Build authority through clear educational content.
4. Convert visitors into calculator users and quote requests.
5. Earn AdSense revenue from informational traffic.

---

## Keyword Strategy

### High-Intent Keyword Types
Focus on terms like:
- solar subsidy + state
- solar calculator + state
- solar system cost + size
- rooftop solar + city
- solar savings calculator
- solar panel price for home
- PM Surya Ghar + state

### Long-Tail Opportunities
Examples:
- 3 kW solar system cost in Bihar
- rooftop solar subsidy in Bihar
- solar panel price for 2 bhk home
- how much roof area for 3 kW solar
- how to apply for solar subsidy in India
- solar savings calculator for home

### Search Intent Buckets
- Informational: learn what solar is
- Comparison: compare system sizes or costs
- Transactional: request quotes
- Local: search for city or state-specific info

Prioritize pages that lead to calculator usage or quote requests.

---

## Content Types

### 1) Calculator Landing Pages
Each calculator should have:
- a clear title,
- a short explanation,
- input fields,
- the result,
- and an FAQ section.

### 2) State Guides
Write one guide per state covering:
- subsidy overview,
- common system sizes,
- local installation process,
- trust checklist,
- and installer discovery.

### 3) FAQ Pages
Answer simple questions people actually search:
- How much solar do I need?
- How much can I save?
- Is subsidy available?
- How do I choose an installer?
- What documents do I need?

### 4) Comparison Pages
Examples:
- 1 kW vs 3 kW solar
- rooftop solar vs no solar
- subsidy vs no subsidy
- bill-based estimate vs roof-based estimate

---

## On-Page SEO Rules

- Use one primary keyword per page.
- Put the keyword in the title, H1, and early intro naturally.
- Use short paragraphs.
- Use descriptive headings.
- Include internal links to calculators and related guides.
- Add FAQ sections where useful.
- Avoid keyword stuffing.
- Avoid generic filler text.

---

## Content Tone

The content should feel:
- helpful,
- calm,
- trustworthy,
- and non-salesy.

Do not write like a brochure.
Write like a guide that helps a homeowner make a decision.

---

## Internal Linking Strategy

Every major article should link to:
- the relevant calculator,
- the quote request form,
- and a related state guide.

Example flow:
Guide → Calculator → Results → Installer listing → Quote request

This is the ideal conversion path.

---

## AdSense-Friendly Content Strategy

Use ads primarily on:
- informational guides,
- FAQs,
- state pages,
- educational articles,
- and high-traffic calculator result pages.

Do not make ad placement aggressive.
A clean site will usually perform better long term than a cluttered one.

---

## SEO Technical Requirements

- Fast page load.
- Mobile-friendly layouts.
- Clean metadata.
- Proper page titles and descriptions.
- Crawlable HTML.
- Structured content.
- Simple URL structure.
- Avoid heavy JavaScript where possible on content pages.

---

## Metadata Rules

Every page should have:
- a unique title,
- a clear meta description,
- one H1,
- and logical H2/H3 structure.

Titles should be direct and useful.

Examples:
- Solar Subsidy in Bihar
- 3 kW Solar System Cost in Bihar
- Solar Savings Calculator for Indian Homes

---

## FAQ SEO Rule

Add an FAQ section to pages where it helps:
- boost usefulness,
- answer search intent,
- and support rich results potential.

Keep FAQs short and direct.

---

## Content Scaling Strategy

Start with Bihar.
Once the Bihar pages perform, replicate the structure for:
- Jharkhand
- Uttar Pradesh
- other states

Do not rewrite the whole site for every state.
Use a repeatable content template and customize state-specific details.

---

## Quality Rules

Do not publish pages that:
- say nothing useful,
- repeat what every other site says,
- or exist only to target a keyword.

Every page should help a real person decide whether solar is right for their home.
